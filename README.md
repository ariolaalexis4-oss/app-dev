# My Favorite Movie: Spider-Man

## About the Movie
**Spider-Man** is a superhero movie about Peter Parker, a young man who gains amazing abilities after being bitten by a radioactive spider. He uses his powers to protect people and fight criminals.

## Main Characters
- Peter Parker (Spider-Man)
- Mary Jane Watson
- Aunt May
- Green Goblin

## Why I Like This Movie
I like this movie because it has exciting action scenes, an interesting story, and amazing superhero moments. It also teaches the importance of responsibility, courage, and helping others.

## My Favorite Quote
> "With great power comes great responsibility."

## Rating
**10/10** – Highly Recommended!
